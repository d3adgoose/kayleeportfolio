Production and Game Design

I’m a producer/game designer dedicated to accessibility, representation, and cognitive inclusion. I love bringing teams and creative ideas together through production and game design. My background in event planning taught me how to coordinate people, schedules, logistics, and unexpected changes. I bring that same care to milestones, sprints, and collaborative game development.

Education and Values

I’m currently pursuing my Master’s in Computer Science with a focus on Game Development at the University of Southern California, where I am a recipient of the Gerald A. Lawson USC Games Fund Supported by PlayStation Career Pathways. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science: Game Design from the University of California, Santa Cruz. Drawing from experience in product management, Scrum, software development, and UI/UX, I build experiences that resonate widely and invite more players to feel seen in play.

Where I’m headed

I’m pursuing producer roles where I can support healthy teams, clear pipelines, and thoughtful player experiences. Game Design is my other core focus.
